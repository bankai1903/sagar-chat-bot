Q. What are the eligibility criteria for B.Tech admission?
## Answer
For admission in B. Tech, students must have passed 10+2 or equivalent exam in Science Stream (PCM/PCB) from a Recognized Board/Council/University either State or National or International.
The minimum aggregate marks should be 50% in the case of General and 45% in the case of SC/ST/OBC.
Q. Are XII pass-out students from the NIOS board and Bihar Open Board eligible for admission in B.Tech.?
## Answer
Yes, all educational boards are accepted for admission in B. Tech. as long as they are recognized by the Government of India.
Q. On which basis can I apply for admission to B.Tech?
## Answer
Students can apply for admission to B. Tech. based on the following:
* JEE Mains - for 1st-year admission based on JEE Score 2026
* Qualifying Exam Based - for 1st-year admission based on (10+2) result
Note: JEE Score will be given priority when compared to the 12th result
Q. How many rounds are there in MP DTE 2026 counseling?
## Answer
There are two major rounds in MP DTE 2026 counselling.
* Round 1 – Based on JEE Score
* Round 2 – Based on JEE Score (for seats left blank after 1st round)
Once these two rounds finish, students can go for College Level Counseling (CLC) for the admission process.
Q. Is it necessary to register first in MP DTE Counselling to get admitted to college?
## Answer
Yes it is very much necessary to register first in MP DTE Counseling to gain admission to college
Q. What are the percentage criteria for admission to college?
## Answer
The student must have passed his 12th exam in Science Stream from a recognized Board/University/Council with a minimum of 50% score for general candidates and a minimum of 45% score in the case of SC/ST/OBC candidates.
Q. What is the best method to take admission in college?
## Answer
The best and proper method while taking admission in a college is to visit the college campus personally and take active notes on the workings of the institute. Necessary questions about attendance, lab workings, and curriculum should also be asked. Meeting with the department and college head should also be done as a precautionary measure.

## Answer
SISTec offers NBA-accredited engineering programs in CSE, EC, EX, ME, and CE, ensuring high-quality education. With the BSCC scheme, eligible students can cover their tuition fees, books, and academic expenses while studying at one of the top colleges in Bhopal with the Bihar Credit Card facility.
Q. What is the eligibility to apply for a Bihar Student Credit Card at SISTec?
## Answer
To avail of the BSCC facility at SISTec, students must:
* Be a resident of Bihar
* Have passed Class 12 (PCM stream) from a recognized board
* Secure admission in NBA-accredited programs at SISTec
* Be aged up to 25 years at the time of application
SISTec is a recognized Bihar Credit Card College in Bhopal, making it easier for Bihar students to pursue engineering with financial support.
Q. What is the process to apply for a Bihar Student Credit Card at SISTec?
## Answer
Eligible students can follow these steps:
* Secure admission to SISTec, an NBA College in Bhopal
* Apply online at www.7nishchay-yuvaupmission.bihar.gov.in
* Upload necessary documents (Aadhar, 10+2 mark sheet, college admission proof, etc.)
* Await approval and loan disbursement
SISTec is among the top colleges in Bhopal with a Bihar Credit Card facility, ensuring smooth education financing for Bihar students.

SISTec offers NBA-accredited engineering programs in CSE, EC, EX, ME, and CE, ensuring high-quality education. With the BSCC scheme, eligible students can cover their tuition fees, books, and academic expenses while studying at one of the top colleges in Bhopal with the Bihar Credit Card facility.
Q. What is the eligibility to apply for a Bihar Student Credit Card at SISTec?
## Answer
To avail of the BSCC facility at SISTec, students must:
* Be a resident of Bihar
* Have passed Class 12 (PCM stream) from a recognized board
* Secure admission in NBA-accredited programs at SISTec
* Be aged up to 25 years at the time of application
SISTec is a recognized Bihar Credit Card College in Bhopal, making it easier for Bihar students to pursue engineering with financial support.
