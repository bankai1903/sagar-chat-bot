## 1. Student Activity Council (SAC) Structure & Internal Committees

### Centralized SAC Architecture & Four Core Committees
The Student Activity Council (SAC) is the apex student governing body at SISTec across Gandhi Nagar and Ratibad campuses. SAC is organized into four core internal student committees:
1. **Art and Cultural Committee**: Organizes dance competitions, musical concerts, theatrical drama, Nukkad Natak, festive celebrations, and fests.
2. **Sports Committee**: Coordinates inter-departmental, inter-collegiate tournaments, athletic meets, and Sagar Reunion sports leagues.
3. **Placement Committee**: Coordinates with Corporate Relations during campus recruitment drives, student batch logistics, and company coordination.
4. **Digital and Photography Committee**: Oversees event media coverage, photography, cinematography, graphic design, and social media outreach.
- **Leadership Development**: Empowers students with event planning, teamwork, and communication skills.

## 3. Sagar Euphoria & Kala Utsav (कला उत्सव)

### Creative Expression & Artistic Talent
- **Sagar Euphoria**:
  - Dedicated annual cultural festival bringing together students from engineering, pharmacy, and management.
  - Features vocal music, instrumentals, group choreography, street plays, mimicry, and stand-up comedy.
  - Provides a vibrant creative platform for students to step away from academic routines and discover their talents.
- **Kala Utsav (कला उत्सव)**:
  - Annual art, culture, and creativity celebration organized by the Student Activity Council.
  - Showcases student creativity in live canvas painting, sketching, digital illustration, origami, and handicraft exhibitions.
  - Fills the campus with artistic installations, vibrant colors, and cultural pride.

## 4. Sagar Jalsa & Navratri Mahotsav (Garba & Dandiya Celebrations)

### Grand Navratri Cultural Extravaganzas
- **Sagar Jalsa**:
  - Grand annual Navratri Garba and Dandiya night organized by the Student Activity Council (SAC) in collaboration with departmental student associations (FACE and TAES).
  - Over 500+ students and faculty participate in traditional ethnic attire, dancing to rhythmic Garba beats with a live DJ and dhol orchestra.
  - Includes structured pre-event Garba practice workshops organized across the week.
- **Navratri Mahotsav @ SISTec Ratibad**:
  - Inter-branch Garba dance competitions with ceremonial lamp lighting and traditional puja.
  - Best Dressed (Student & Faculty) and Best Dancer awards presented to celebrate enthusiasm and cultural unity.

## 5. Festive Celebrations: Kalakriti, Utopia & Campus Orientation

### Traditional Celebrations & Induction Events
- **SISTec Kalakriti**:
  - Annual Diwali celebration organized by SAC featuring artistic rangoli competitions and diya decoration.
  - Highlight includes a grand geometric diya formation of 300+ earthen diyas illuminated at the campus 'Creations' arena by students and dignitaries.
- **UTOPIA**:
  - Multi-art youth talent carnival organized at SISTec Ratibad featuring singing, poetry slams, sketching, artificial tattoo designing, and logical games.
- **Know Your Campus**:
  - Signature treasure-hunt orientation event organized exclusively for first-year hostel students.
  - Teams solve cryptic campus clues within a 30-minute window to discover laboratories, libraries, and amenities across the institute.

## 6. Academic Competitions, Literary Events & Social Service

### Inter-Disciplinary Forums & National Days
- **Prastuti**:
  - Annual flagship presentation competition organized by SAC covering trending technologies: Artificial Intelligence, IoT, Blockchain, Hologram Tech, Machine Learning, and Quantum Computing.
  - Evaluated on technical depth, presentation style, and Q&A defense.
- **National Youth Day & Literary Contests**:
  - Jointly organized by SAC and the National Service Scheme (NSS) unit celebrating Swami Vivekananda's birth anniversary.
  - Includes youth storytelling, Hindi & English poetry recitation, debates, and elocution on national building.
- **Social Awareness Campaigns**:
  - Blood donation drives, campus tree plantations, cleanliness drives, and community outreach organized under NSS and SAC social wings.
