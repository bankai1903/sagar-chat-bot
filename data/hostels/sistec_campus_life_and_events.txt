# SISTec Bhopal - Campus Life, Clubs, Events & Annual Fests

## Student Life & Campus Culture
Life at SISTec combines rigorous academic pursuit with vibrant co-curricular, technical, and cultural activities managed by students and faculty:

## Student Activity Council (SAC)
The Student Activity Council (SAC) is the apex student governing body responsible for organizing campus initiatives, clubs, sports, and cultural festivals under the guidance of faculty convenors.

## Key Cultural & Technical Fests
1. **Sagar Fiesta**:
   - The flagship annual national-level techno-cultural festival of Sagar Group of Institutions.
   - Features celebrity performances, rock band concerts, fashion shows, dance face-offs, technical hackathons, robotic wars, and gaming arenas.
2. **Sagar Jalsa & Raasrang**:
   - High-energy traditional Garba and Dandiya festival celebrated with traditional music, dance competitions, and campus feasts during Navratri.
3. **SISTec Innovation Hackathon**:
   - 24 to 36-hour non-stop student hackathon solving real-world challenges in smart agriculture, IoT, AI healthcare, cybersecurity, and clean energy.
4. **Mini-Anveshana & Nirman**:
   - Annual science and engineering project exposition where student innovations and working hardware prototypes are evaluated by corporate judges.
5. **Tejomay Awards**:
   - Annual prestige awards honoring students and faculty for exceptional academic, research, sports, and social service contributions.

## Student Clubs & Societies
- **Google Developer Student Club (GDSC)**: Developer meetups, hackathons, and open-source contributions.
- **IEEE Student Branch**: Technical symposiums, paper presentations, and international webinar series.
- **CSI (Computer Society of India) Chapter**: Coding bootcamps, web development contests, and algorithmic challenges.
- **SAE India Collegiate Club**: Designing and fabricating all-terrain vehicles (ATVs) for national BAJA and SUPRA competitions.
- **Rotaract Club & CSR Society**: Blood donation camps, tree plantation drives, and rural teaching initiatives.
- **Literary & Debating Society**: Model United Nations (MUN), debates, creative writing, and poetry slams.
- **Sports Club**: Coordinates inter-college leagues in cricket, football, basketball, badminton, table tennis, and athletics.
