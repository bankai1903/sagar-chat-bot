# SISTec Bhopal - Campus Facilities, Hostels, Transport & Amenities

## Campuses Overview
SISTec has two modern campuses in Bhopal:
1. **SISTec Gandhi Nagar (Main Campus)**:
   - Location: Opposite Raja Bhoj International Airport, Gandhi Nagar, Bhopal (M.P.) - 462036.
   - Houses Engineering, MBA, and Pharmacy faculties.
2. **SISTec Ratibad Campus (SISTec-R)**:
   - Location: Sikandrabad, Near Ratibad, Bhadbhada Road, Bhopal (M.P.) - 462044.
   - Picturesque green campus equipped with engineering faculties, labs, and sports arenas.

## Residential & Hostel Facilities
- **Separate Hostels**: Dedicated secure hostels for boys and girls on campus premises.
- **Room Types & Occupancy**:
  - 2-bed shared rooms
  - 3-bed shared rooms
  - 4-bed and 5-bed shared options
- **Hostel Fee (2026 Official)**:
  - Flat ₹1,00,000 per academic year for both Gandhi Nagar (GN) and Ratibad (RB) campuses. Includes furnished room, 24/7 electricity backup, Wi-Fi, and complete vegetarian mess charges.
- **Room Amenities**:
  - Each student is provided a single bed with mattress, personal study desk, study chair, and individual locked wardrobe/cupboard.
- **Hostel Features**:
  - 24/7 security with CCTV surveillance and security guards stationed at entry/exit gates.
  - Continuous water supply with water purification systems (RO drinking water).
  - High-speed campus Wi-Fi access in hostel rooms.
  - Power backup with heavy-duty diesel generators ensuring uninterrupted electricity.
  - Indoor recreation room with TV, table tennis, carrom, and chess.
  - Resident warden and faculty in-charge staying on premises for student welfare and discipline.
  - Night curfew rules: Hostellers are required to be back in their respective hostels by the designated evening roll-call time (typically 7:30 PM - 8:00 PM unless prior formal permission is granted).

## Mess & Dining
- Spacious, hygienic dining halls serving balanced, nutritious vegetarian meals.
- Four meals provided daily: Breakfast, Lunch, Evening snacks/tea, and Dinner.
- Student mess committee meets regularly with administration to review and update weekly menus.

## Campus Transport / Bus Services
- **Fleet**: Dedicated fleet of over 100 college buses.
- **City Coverage**: Comprehensive routes covering all key areas, residential neighborhoods, and transit hubs across Bhopal (including MP Nagar, Kolar, BHEL, Hoshangabad Road, Old City, Bairagarh, Airport Road, TT Nagar, Ayodhya Bypass, Indrapuri).
- **Safety**: GPS-tracked buses with verified drivers and staff attendants. Available for day-scholar students and faculty.

## Sports & Fitness Facilities
- Outdoor sports grounds for Cricket, Football, Volleyball, Basketball, and Athletics.
- Indoor facilities for Badminton, Table Tennis, and Chess.
- On-campus gymnasium equipped with modern cardio and strength training equipment.
- Annual sports fest ("Sagar Sangram" / sports tournaments) encouraging university-level participation.

## Library & Learning Resources
- Fully computerized Central Library with vast collection of over 40,000 books, reference volumes, national and international journals, and research publications.
- Digital Library with DELNET, IEEE subscriptions, and e-book access.
- Dedicated air-conditioned reading halls open during extended college hours and examination periods.

## Healthcare & Medical Support
- On-campus first-aid center and emergency medical dispensary with visiting doctor.
- Immediate tie-ups with nearby multi-specialty hospitals in Bhopal and dedicated campus ambulance availability for emergency transport.
