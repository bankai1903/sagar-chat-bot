# SISTec Bhopal - Academics, Programs & Examination

## Overview & Affiliations
- **Institution**: Sagar Institute of Science and Technology (SISTec), Bhopal.
- **Affiliated University**: Rajiv Gandhi Proudyogiki Vishwavidyalaya (RGPV), Bhopal for Engineering, Pharmacy, and MBA programs; Barkatullah University (BU) for selected undergraduate/business courses.
- **Approvals & Accreditations**: Approved by All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), New Delhi and Pharmacy Council of India (PCI). Accredited by NAAC and NBA for eligible programs.

## Undergraduate Programs (B.Tech)
Offered across SISTec Gandhi Nagar and SISTec Ratibad campuses:
1. **Computer Science & Engineering (CSE)**
2. **CSE - Artificial Intelligence & Data Science (AI & DS)**
3. **CSE - Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning (AI & ML)**
4. **CSE - Cyber Security**
5. **CSE - Internet of Things (IoT)**
6. **Computer Science & Information Technology (CSIT)**
7. **Electronics & Communication Engineering (ECE)**
8. **Electrical Engineering (EE)**
9. **Electrical & Electronics Engineering (EEE)**
10. **Mechanical Engineering (ME)**
11. **Civil Engineering (CE)**

- **Duration**: 4 Years (8 Semesters)
- **Degree Awarded**: Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) affiliated to RGPV Bhopal.
- **Curriculum**: Follows RGPV Choice Based Credit System (CBCS) syllabus with mandatory industrial internships, minor and major projects, and continuous assessment.

## Postgraduate Engineering (M.Tech)
- **Duration**: 2 Years (4 Semesters)
- **Specializations**:
  - Computer Science & Engineering
  - VLSI Design
  - Digital Communication
  - Power System Engineering
  - Structural Engineering
  - Construction Technology
  - Thermal Power Engineering
  - Machine Design

## Pharmacy Programs (SIPTec - Sagar Institute of Pharmacy and Technology)
- **B.Pharm (Bachelor of Pharmacy)**: 4-Year degree program approved by PCI and affiliated to RGPV. Focuses on pharmaceutical chemistry, pharmacology, pharmacognosy, pharmaceutics, and drug regulatory affairs.
- **M.Pharm (Master of Pharmacy)**: 2-Year postgraduate program with specializations in:
  - Pharmaceutics
  - Pharmaceutical Chemistry

## Management Programs (SISTec Business School)
- **MBA (Master of Business Administration)**: 2-Year full-time degree affiliated to RGPV / Barkatullah University.
  - Specializations: Marketing, Finance, Human Resource Management (HRM), Banking & Financial Services, Retail, Business Analytics.
- **BBA (Bachelor of Business Administration)**: 3-Year undergraduate foundation course in management.

## Academic Calendar & Examination Rules
- **Semester System**: Two semesters per academic year (Odd semester: July to December; Even semester: January to June).
- **Attendance Requirement**: Minimum 75% attendance in theory and lab sessions is mandatory as per RGPV university norms to be eligible for university examinations.
- **Evaluation**: Continuous Internal Assessment (Mid-semester tests, quizzes, assignments, lab evaluations) carries 30-40% weightage; End-Semester University Exams conducted by RGPV carry 60-70% weightage.
- **Grading System**: 10-point SGPA (Semester Grade Point Average) and CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average) scale.
