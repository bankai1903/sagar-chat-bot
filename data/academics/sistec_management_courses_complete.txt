## 2A. MBA Dual Specialization System (Core Functional Domains)

### Marketing, HR & Finance Specializations
SISTec Business School offers full-time Dual Specialization MBA. Students choose two specializations in Sem III:
- **Marketing Management**:
  - Focuses on consumer psychology, branding, advertising, and market analysis.
  - Key Subjects: Consumer Behaviour, Advertisement Management, Sales & Distribution Management, Service Marketing, and Digital Marketing.
- **Human Resource Management (HRM)**:
  - Focuses on talent acquisition, labor laws, training, and strategic human capital.
  - Key Subjects: Industrial Relations, Training & Development, HR Planning, Compensation Management.
- **Financial Management (Finance)**:
  - Focuses on capital structure, portfolio management, valuation, and advisory.
  - Key Subjects: Security Analysis & Investment Management, Working Capital, Strategic Corporate Finance.

## 6B. DTE Admission Process, CMAT & Counseling Stages

### MP DTE Centralized Online Counseling
Admission to MBA and BBA at SISTec Business School follows Directorate of Technical Education (DTE) MP rules:
1. Online Registration on MP DTE counseling portal.
2. Verification of candidate academic documents.
3. Online Choice Filling and locked college preferences with part fee payment.
4. Merit-based seat allotment by DTE MP.
5. In-person reporting at SISTec Business School for physical document verification and admission.

### CMAT Preference & College Level Counseling (CLC)
- **CMAT Priority**: Candidates appearing in CMAT (Common Management Admission Test) are counseled with first priority in Round 1.
- **Direct / CLC Round**: Non-CMAT candidates can secure admission during College Level Counseling (CLC) based on graduation merit on a first-come, first-served basis.

## 7. Examination System, Passing Rules & Scholarships

### Evaluation Scheme (Barkatullah University Pattern)
- **Total Marks per Subject**: 100 Marks (20 Marks Internal + 80 Marks External University Exam).
- **Minimum Passing Marks**:
  - Internal Exam: Minimum 8 marks out of 20.
  - External University Exam: Minimum 32 marks out of 80.
  - Subject Aggregate: Minimum 50% overall marks (combined internal + external) required to pass each subject.
- **Attendance Mandate**: Minimum 75% attendance is strictly compulsory for regular semester progression.

### Government Scholarships (Post-Matric / DTE MP)
Eligible reserved category candidates belonging to Madhya Pradesh receive government scholarships:
- **SC / ST Candidates**: Approximately INR 60,000 per year scholarship.
- **OBC Candidates**: Approximately INR 32,500 per year scholarship.
- **Mandatory Documents**: MP Domicile Certificate, Caste Certificate, Parents Income Certificate, Aadhar Card, and Bank Passbook.

## 9. Summer Internship Program (SIP), Placements & Recruiters

### Summer Internship Program (SIP)
- Mandatory 6 to 8-week corporate internship during the summer break between Semester II and Semester III.
- Dual mentorship architecture: Faculty Mentor from SISTec + Corporate Guide from the hiring company.
- Evaluation requires formal Summer Internship Report, employer performance feedback, and viva-voce.

### Placement Track Record & Top Recruiters
- **Batch 2023-25 Highlights**: 210 Job Offers, 92% Internship Placement Rate, 20+ Corporate MoUs, 40+ Campus Hiring Drives.
- **Highest Salary Package**: INR 10 LPA (offered by Intellipaat).
- **Average Salary Package**: INR 4.5 LPA.
- **Top Recruiters**: Intellipaat, Amazon (via IIEC), Reliance Retail, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Axis Bank, Flipkart, TCS, Extramarks, Byju's, S&P Global, Sagar Group Conglomerate Enterprises (Agrawal Builders, Sagar Manufacturers, Sagar Nutriments).
