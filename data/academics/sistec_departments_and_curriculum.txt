## B.Tech in Computer Science &
Engineering
Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) is an integrated academic program combining the fields of
computer engineering and computer science, focusing on the digital electronics domain with added
courses in computer architecture, processor design, operating systems, high-performance
computing,
parallel processing, computer networks, and embedded systems.
The CSE program at SISTec focuses on practical exposure along with technology. Computer Science
&
Engineering also includes core subjects of computer science such as the theory of computation,
design & analysis of algorithms, data structures, and database systems. Hands-on practices on
RGPV
subjects like Operating System, DBMS, Computer Network, Software Engineering, Cloud Computing,
and
Soft Computing are part of the industry-oriented teaching modules that students work on at Sagar
Group of Institutions.
The course program works on a 4x4 customized study module for better learning where four theory,
four practical classes are conducted as opposed to standard two theory, two practical classes.
As
part of its focus on industry requirements, the department imparts special sessions on Python,
JAVA, Web Designing, and Android App Development with live major-minor project making under the
able guidance of trained faculties. Seven active labs on DSA/ADA Labs, DBMS/DOT NET Lab,
Software
Lab, Machine Learning Lab, Java Programming Lab, Language Lab, Basic Computer Programming/Matlab
Lab provide all the required training based on students’ passion.
Amongst all the engineering branches, Computer Science is the most popular choice among
students.
This branch of engineering is perceived to be popular due to its research scopes and is known to
be
challenging while offering good career opportunities and remuneration. Big Basket, Infosys,
Accenture, SAP Labs, TCS, Wipro, Cognizant, Praemineo, and Hexaware Technologies are some of the
popular recruiters visiting Sagar Group of Institutions for campus drives.
Computer Science and Engineering graduates can make a career in various areas like web
applications, computer graphics, video games, enterprise computing, embedded systems, network
administration, mobile applications, computer vision, scientific modeling, computer security,
database systems, animation, wireless network, artificial intelligence, computational skills,
computer manufacturing, computer programming, computational biology, supercomputing to name a
few.
Almost all industries have job profiles for computer engineers hence the increasing demand for
them.
Apart from core subjects such as Object-Oriented Programming, Data Structures, Computer
Architecture, Analysis and Design of Algorithms, Operating Systems, Compiler Design, and
Software
Engineering, the course curriculum imbibes an interdisciplinary approach. Subjects such as
Microprocessors, Digital Circuits, and Systems have great practical utility and demand in the
industry. Students can also opt for inter-disciplinary subjects as electives. The department
also
offers an M.Tech curriculum in the Computer Science stream as its post-graduate initiative.
## M.Tech in Computer Science &
Engineering
Technology is omnipresent in our lives and with a boom in the technology sector within these
past few decades, it is proving to be a lucrative career opportunity for students in the 21st
Century especially those who are at the juncture to pass out of school and are considering
various aspects for their future career option. Computer Science Engineering is the most popular
course for engineering aspirants. Even during the counselling of the top engineering colleges,
CSE seats are most demanded when compared with different branches.
Sagar Institute Bhopal offers M.Tech. program in Computer Science & Engineering. This program is
designed to provide in-depth and advanced study of various aspects of Computer Science and
Engineering like Soft Computing, Network Security, Mobile Ad-Hoc Networking, Data Mining, and
Data Warehousing. In addition, there are specialized courses like High-Performance Computing,
Big Data, and Geographical Information System which provide the necessary analytical and
technical competence among engineers in this area. A Masters Degree in CSE can not only be
utilized for research purposes but also in garnering industry experience.
The department has well-qualified, highly motivated, and fully committed faculty to impart
quality education at both undergraduate and postgraduate levels. Most of the teaching staff is
either Ph.D. or pursuing the work for their Ph.D. The department is well equipped with computer
labs with the latest computing systems, technology, and round a clock Internet facility. The
program includes seminars and a thesis that helps students develop basic concepts and
intellectual competence.

Intelligence and Data Science
Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the latest technological advancement in the field of automation
and
it is the process of simulating advanced intelligence / human intelligence in machines to think
and
mimic like humans. As the name suggests artificial intelligence is recreated to suit a user’s
demand.
Data Science on the other hand is the interdisciplinary field that uses certain scientific
methods,
processes, and algorithms to extract knowledge and insights from structured and unstructured
data,
and apply knowledge and actionable insights from data across a broad range of application
domains.
Artificial Intelligence and Data Science go hand-in-hand in a way that will prepare the students
with the skills to perform data analysis and based on that knowledge create AI-powered programs
and
applications. AI and Data Science in recent years have become a trending topic and one of the
most
sought-after fields with high-paying, dynamic, and thriving careers in technology.
B.Tech in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science is a 4-year undergraduate engineering degree
program. The students are taught the core technologies of artificial intelligence, data mining,
and
data modeling. In addition to this, the students will gain cross-disciplinary skills in
statistics,
logical reasoning, mathematical reasoning, knowledge discovery, and visualization skills.
In order to fully understand the concepts of B.Tech in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science
certain important topics will be covered such as Augmented Reality & Virtual Reality, Deep
Learning, Introduction to Data Science, Data Visualization, Natural Language Processing,
Programming for Problem Solving, Python Programming, Data Structures and Algorithms, Database
Management Systems, Object-Oriented Programming, and many more.
After completing this program, students can venture into this field at various profiles of
Business
Analyst, Data Analyst, Intelligence Analyst, Data Manager, Information Security Analyst, Risk
Analyst, etc. The biggest technological players of the industry are always looking for competent
engineers and companies such as Adani, Accenture Analytics, Adobe, Airbus, Amazon, BYJUs,
Capgemini, Cognizant, Dassault Systemes, HCL Technologies, Hexaware, MuSigma, Paypal, SAP, Tata
Consultancy Services, etc are recruiting actively and many of these players have previously
visited
Sagar Colleges Bhopal for active recruitment for various profiles.

## B.Tech in Artificial
Intelligence and Machine Learning
Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the latest technological advancement in the field of automation
and
it is the process of simulating advanced intelligence / human intelligence in machines to think
and
mimic humans. As the name suggests artificial intelligence is recreated to suit a user’s demand.
Machine Learning is a part of Artificial Intelligence where through computer algorithms machines
are taught to learn and evolve their working automatically through user experience and data.
B.Tech in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning (AI-ML) is a 4-year undergraduate
engineering degree program. The students of AI-ML are taught to write code and program the
machines
through which they can advance to think and act like human beings. This coding and programming
is
the essence or we can say the soul of the machines, working as guiding instruction for them. The
said codes and programs which are somewhat like the mother tongue to the machines will help them
in
performing tasks with minimal human intervention.
In order to write these codes and programs, the students will be taught certain programming
languages, understood by the machines, like Prolog, Lisp, JAVA, and Python. Essentially, this
B.Tech undergraduate program is centered around the programming of machines through computer
languages. Hence in this program, the students are required to learn and practice these specific
languages, used to customize the functionalities of machines.
After completing the B.Tech in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning program, students
can
venture into this field at various profiles of Big Data Engineer, Business Intelligence
Developer,
Machine Learning Engineer, AI Data Analyst, Product Manager, AI Engineer, Robotics Engineer,
etc.
The requirement of students competent in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning is
skyrocketing with the advent of the internet, new technologies, and a new age of automation. The
biggest companies in the global market revolve around programming and top players like Amazon,
Accenture, SAP, Adobe, Persistent Systems, WhiteHat Jr, Mahindra, Deloitte, Tata Communications,
Cognizant, Genpact, Capgemini, Wipro, etc may hire individuals who are passed out form this
undergraduate course. Many of these players have already visited the Sagar Group of Institutions
for active recruitment.

## B.Tech in Computer Science &
Engineering with Internet of Things
The Internet of Things (IoT) can be described as a network of physical objects (things),
embedded
with certain sensors, software, and other technologies with the purpose to connect and exchange
data with other devices and systems over the Internet. This technology mostly can be seen in
smart
homes or home automation where various devices and appliances are connected to a central network
and can be controlled via devices such as smartphones and smart speakers. It is a full system of
multiple digital machines transformed into interconnected computing devices.
B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering with Internet of Things is a 4-year undergraduate
engineering degree program. Students opting for this program will study all the essential core
Computer Science & Engineering courses and in addition, will study electives related to the
Internet of Things. Upon completion of the program, students shall have a thorough knowledge of
computer science and a firm grip on wireless communication, computer networks, IoT devices,
systems, and networking.
After completing this program, students can venture into this field with profiles of IoT
Engineer,
IoT App Developer, IoT Solution Architect, IoT Devices System Administrator, and IoT Scientist
to
name a few.

## B.Tech in Electronics &
Communication Engineering
Electronics and Communications Engineering (ECE) is the engineering stream involved in the
research, design, development, and testing of electronic equipment used in various electric
systems. This stream of engineering mainly deals with analog transmissions, basic electronics,
microprocessors, digital and analog communication, integrated circuits, microwave engineering,
satellite communications, antennae, and wave progressions.
The ECE department at SISTec offers B.Tech and M.Tech course programs in Electronics &
Communication Engineering with a focus on the latest trends and technology training so that our
budding engineers can prove themselves outside in the corporate world. The department works with
an
end focus on reducing industry and institute gaps, thus special attention is given to subjects
like
Electronic Devices / Microprocessor / VLSI / TV and Mobile Communications.
PCB Designing, Embedded, VLSI, JAVA, Web Page Designing, IoT, Robotics Design, Industrial VLSI
Design, and Arduino modules are certain extra training given to students apart from the regular
curriculum. The department also has an e-yantra Robotics Lab sponsored by MHRD through the
National
Mission of Education - ICT in collaboration with IIT Bombay. Students have also prepared
projects
in the laboratories under the guidance of faculties. Smart Lecture Monitoring System using
Arduino,
Online Book Tracking System, Message Display using Bluetooth, Talking Gloves, and Smart Parking
System are a few of the advanced projects made.
The department has experienced and dedicated faculties with a strong commitment to engineering
education maintaining its streak to be on a growing path. Faculties have published their
research
work in reputed journals/conferences. Because of the Digital India initiative by the Indian
Government and the subsequent enhancement of electronic chip technology, the department of ECE
is
committed to providing in-depth theoretical knowledge and skilled practical training in these
emerging fields of Electronics & Communication Engineering. The department’s main focus is on
the
overall practical exposure to the students on various aspects of Electronics and Communication
Engineering. Frequent industrial visits and project-based assignments are some of the
departmental
incessant activities.
## M.Tech in Digital
Communication
M.Tech. in Digital Communication is a program that focuses on the development, use, critical
evaluation, and regulation of new electronic communication technologies using computer
applications; and that prepares individuals to function as developers and managers of digital
communications media. It includes instructions in computer and telecommunications technologies
and processes; design and development of digital communications; marketing and distribution;
digital communications regulation, law, and policy; the study of human interaction with, and use
of, digital media; and emerging trends and issues.
The M.Tech course of Digital Communication aims at providing in-depth knowledge to students of
Electronics and Telecommunication background in various subjects of present-day interest like
Advanced Digital Communication. The program aims to provide students with a wide variety of
opportunities in Research and Development (R&D) and industry experience.
Modern DSP, Advanced Embedded System, Antenna Theory and Design, Digital Electronics, Error
control and coding, Advanced Computer Architecture, along with a course on Linear Algebra and
elective courses like CMOS VLSI Design, Wireless & Mobile Network, Advances in VLSI design,
Real-Time Operating System are some of the options to select for students according to subjects
of their interest.
## M.Tech in Very Large-Scale
Integration (VLSI) Design
Very Large-Scale Integration Design or VLSI Design is based on current industry standards that
help attendees to secure placements in their dream jobs. VLSI Design course involves "Learning
by Doing" using state-of-the-art infrastructure for performing hands-on exercises and real-world
simulations. This extensive hands-on experience in VLSI Designing training ensures that you
absorb the knowledge and skills that you will need to apply at work after your placement in an
MNC.
The M.Tech in VLSI is a 2-years Masters's Degree Programme for students interested in the
current and expanding field of VLSI circuit design, to equip students with detailed knowledge of
the latest technologies and the skills required to apply this knowledge in the design of VLSI
systems. This program is designed to prepare students for leading-edge positions in the industry
in the areas of VLSI. The program covers engineering aspects in designing and developing
IC-based systems.
The program curriculum is aimed at VLSI System Design: Hardware description Languages, System
Architectures, Physical designs, Verification techniques, Simulation & Synthesis, Low power
design techniques, Mixed-mode design methodologies, and Embedded System Design: Advanced
Embedded Microcontrollers, Real-Time Embedded Systems, Advanced Embedded System Design, and
System on Chip.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
Q. What is the eligibility for the BBA program at SISTec?
## Answer
Students who have passed 12th grade from any recognized board (any stream) are eligible to apply for the BBA program at SISTec.
Q. Which BBA specializations are offered at SISTec?
## Answer
SISTec offers three career-focused BBA specializations:
* Marketing
* Finance
* Human Resources (HR)
These are designed to match current industry needs and student interests.
Q. Is the BBA degree from SISTec recognized?
## Answer
Yes, the BBA program at SISTec Business School is affiliated with Barkatullah University, a recognized state university.
Q. Does SISTec offer internships during the BBA course?
## Answer
Absolutely! SISTec has a Structured Summer Internship Program (SIP) that gives students hands-on industry experience before graduation.
Q. What kind of placement support does SISTec offer for BBA students?
## Answer
SISTec has a strong placement cell that organizes 100+ campus drives annually. Students also receive training in mock interviews, group discussions, and aptitude tests to ensure they are job-ready.
Q. Are there any extra skill-based certifications with the BBA program?
## Answer
Yes, students get access to national-level certification courses in areas like digital marketing, financial modeling, data analytics, and more.
Q. What is the duration of the BBA course?
## Answer
The BBA program is a 3-year full-time course divided into 6 semesters.
Q. What makes SISTec’s BBA different from others?
## Answer
SISTec offers a blend of classroom learning and real-world exposure through internships, live projects, industry talks, advanced tool training, and personalized mentorship—preparing students not just for jobs, but for leadership roles.
