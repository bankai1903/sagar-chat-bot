## 1. Institutional Overview & Campus Identity of SIPTec-R (Ratibad)

### Institutional Overview
Sagar Institute of Pharmaceutical Technology and Research (SIPTec-R) was established in the year 2023 under the aegis of Shri Agrawal Educational & Cultural Society, founded under the dynamic leadership of Shri Sudhir Kumar Agrawal (Founder Chairman, Sagar Group) and Mr. Siddharth Sudhir Agrawal (Managing Director, Sagar Group). Situated in the scenic campus at Sikandrabad, Ratibad, SIPTec-R is the premier pharmacy institution of the Sagar Group dedicated to modern pharmaceutical training.

### Core Philosophy & Healthcare Vision
- SIPTec-R was established with the primary objective of imparting high-quality, practical-driven pharmaceutical education to undergraduate students.
- Emphasis is placed heavily on hands-on experimental workings in laboratories, investing maximum student time in formulation, chemical analysis, and pharmacognostic research.
- The end goal is to enhance the public healthcare system by enabling students to practice the pharmacy profession with clinical competence, ethical responsibility, and technical determination.
- Regular interactions with community pharmacists, clinical researchers, and pharmaceutical industry experts.

### Affiliations, Approvals & Recognitions of SIPTec-R
- **Statutory Council Approval**: Approved by the **Pharmacy Council of India (PCI)**, New Delhi.
- **State Government Approval**: Approved by the **Directorate of Technical Education (DTE)**, Government of Madhya Pradesh.
- **Technical Approval**: Approved by the **All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE)**, New Delhi.
- **University Affiliation**: Affiliated to **Rajiv Gandhi Proudyogiki Vishwavidyalaya (RGPV)**, Bhopal (MP State Technical University).

## 3. Academic Programs & Pharmacy Degrees Offered at SIPTec-R

### Undergraduate Pharmacy Program (B.Pharm - 4 Years / 8 Semesters)
- **Bachelor of Pharmacy (B.Pharm)**:
  - 4-year full-time professional degree program approved by PCI, DTE, AICTE, and affiliated to RGPV Bhopal.
  - Comprehensive syllabus covering Human Anatomy & Physiology, Pharmaceutical Analysis, Pharmaceutics, Inorganic & Organic Pharmaceutical Chemistry, Pharmacognosy, Pharmacology, Pharmaceutical Biotechnology, and Pharmacy Practice.
  - Includes intensive laboratory practicals, hospital pharmacy training, community pharmacy visits, and industrial project work.

### Diploma in Pharmacy (D.Pharm - 2 Years)
- **Diploma in Pharmacy (D.Pharm)**: 2-year foundational technical pharmacy program (applied for at SIPTec-R) aimed at clinical dispensing, retail community pharmacy, and hospital drug management.

## 4. Specialized Infrastructure, Laboratories & Model Pharmacy at SIPTec-R

### Model Pharmacy Facility
- SIPTec-R houses a specialized **Model Pharmacy** on campus.
- Simulates real-world retail and hospital pharmacy setups where students gain experiential training in drug inventory management, prescription auditing, clinical drug dispensing, adverse drug reaction monitoring, and patient counseling.

### Specialized Laboratories & Academic Infrastructure
- **Pharmaceutics & Formulation Lab**: Modern equipment for dosage preparation, liquid orals, ointments, and tablets.
- **Pharmaceutical Chemistry Lab**: Analytical apparatus, fume hoods, reagent benches, and chemical synthesis glassware.
- **Pharmacology Lab**: Diagnostic kits, physiological charts, and computerized simulation software.
- **Pharmacognosy Lab**: Extraction equipment, microtomes, and botanical identification resources.
- **Computer Labs**: High-speed networked terminals with digital pharmaceutical databases.
- **Smart Classrooms & AV Halls**: Interactive multimedia display panels for visual lectures and seminars.

## 5. Publications, Student Life & Industry Placements at SIPTec-R

### Pharmacy E-Magazine: "The Galen"
- SIPTec-R co-publishes **"The Galen"**, an internationally reviewed pharmacy e-magazine alongside SIPTec Gandhi Nagar.
- Features news, research analysis, clinical drug updates, and market reports reviewed by national and international pharmaceutical advisory boards.

### Training, Campus Placements & Amenities
- Students receive structured Campus-to-Corporate (C2C) training in soft skills, corporate aptitude, and regulatory interviews via SGI's centralized T&P Cell.
- Pharmacy placement recruiters include Sun Pharma, Cipla, Lupin, Zydus Lifesciences, Macleods, Bharat Biotech, PharmEasy, and Torrent Pharma.
- Ratibad campus amenities: Residential Hostels for boys and girls with 24/7 security, Wi-Fi, dining mess, sports grounds, conference rooms, and bus connectivity across 100+ routes in Bhopal.
