## 1. Overview of Pharmacy Education at Sagar Group (SIPTec & SIPTec-R)

### Academic Overview & Approvals
Pharmacy education under the Sagar Group of Institutions is offered through two dedicated institutions: Sagar Institute of Pharmacy and Technology (SIPTec, Gandhi Nagar, established 2008) and Sagar Institute of Pharmaceutical Technology and Research (SIPTec-R, Ratibad, established 2023).
- Approved by the Pharmacy Council of India (PCI), New Delhi.
- Approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), New Delhi.
- Affiliated with Rajiv Gandhi Proudyogiki Vishwavidyalaya (RGPV), Bhopal.
- Total Pharmacy Seat Intake across campuses: 290 Seats (B.Pharm 200, M.Pharm 30, D.Pharm 60).
- State-of-the-art pilot plants, Central Instrumentation Facility (CIF), and on-campus Model Pharmacy.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria & Admission Process for Pharmacy Courses

### Bachelor of Pharmacy (B.Pharm - 4 Years) Eligibility & Process
- **Qualification**: 10+2 (Senior Secondary) examination from MP Board, CBSE, ICSE, or recognized state open boards (NIOS/BBOSE).
- **Required Subjects**: Physics and Chemistry as compulsory subjects along with Mathematics or Biology (PCM / PCB stream).
- **Minimum Qualifying Marks**: Minimum 45% aggregate marks for General category; 40% aggregate marks for SC/ST/OBC candidates of Madhya Pradesh.
- **Admission Process**: MP DTE (Directorate of Technical Education) Centralized Online Counseling and College Level Counseling (CLC) rounds based on 10+2 merit.

### B.Pharm Lateral Entry (Direct 2nd Year / 3rd Semester) Eligibility
- **Qualification**: Passed Diploma in Pharmacy (D.Pharm) from an institution approved by the Pharmacy Council of India (PCI) under section 12 of the Pharmacy Act.
- **Minimum Marks**: At least 45% aggregate marks (40% for SC/ST/OBC candidates of Madhya Pradesh).

### Master of Pharmacy (M.Pharm - 2 Years) Eligibility & Process
- **Qualification**: Passed Bachelor of Pharmacy (B.Pharm) degree from an AICTE/PCI-approved institution.
- **Minimum Marks**: Minimum 55% aggregate marks or equivalent CGPA (50% for SC/ST/OBC candidates of MP).
- **Selection Criteria**: Qualified GPAT (Graduate Pharmacy Aptitude Test) scorecard holders given first priority, followed by MP DTE merit counseling.

### Diploma in Pharmacy (D.Pharm - 2 Years) Eligibility
- **Qualification**: Passed 10+2 examination with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology/Mathematics (PCM/PCB) from a recognized board.

## 4. Standardized PCI Curriculum & Laboratory Infrastructure

### Core Subject Modules Across Semesters
- **Foundation & Core Formulation**: Human Anatomy & Physiology, Pharmaceutical Analysis, Physical Pharmaceutics, Inorganic & Organic Pharmaceutical Chemistry, Biochemistry, and Pathophysiology.
- **Advanced Applied Sciences**: Pharmaceutical Microbiology, Pharmaceutical Engineering, Medicinal Chemistry, Pharmacology, Pharmacognosy & Phytochemistry, Industrial Pharmacy, and Biopharmaceutics.
- **Specialized Industry & Clinical Modules**: Novel Drug Delivery Systems (NDDS), Pharmaceutical Biotechnology, Quality Assurance, Regulatory Science, Pharmacy Practice, and Project Dissertation.

### Specialized Practical Labs & Facilities
- **Pharmaceutics Pilot Plant**: Industrial tablet punching, capsule filling, coating pans, and dissolution test stations.
- **Central Instrumentation Facility (CIF)**: UV-Visible Spectrophotometers, HPLC systems, Brookfield Viscometer, Flame Photometer.
- **Model Pharmacy**: Simulated community and hospital pharmacy on campus for prescription auditing, drug dispensing, and patient counseling.
- **Pharmacology Lab & Animal House**: CPCSEA compliant facility for experimental drug screening.
- **Pharmacognosy Lab & Medicinal Garden**: Botanical cultivation and herbal extraction units.
