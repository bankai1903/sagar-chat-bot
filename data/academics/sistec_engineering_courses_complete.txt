## 1. Overview of Engineering Education at SISTec Bhopal

### Academic Overview & Approvals
Engineering education at the Sagar Group of Institutions (SISTec Bhopal) focuses on industry-oriented curriculum delivery, modern laboratory infrastructure, hands-on skill development, and innovative research. Programs are offered at undergraduate (B.Tech) and postgraduate (M.Tech) levels across 3 premier engineering colleges: SISTec (Gandhi Nagar), SISTec-R (Ratibad), and SISTec-E (Ratibad).
- Affiliated with Rajiv Gandhi Proudyogiki Vishwavidyalaya (RGPV), Bhopal.
- Approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), New Delhi.
- NBA and NAAC Accredited programs providing national quality assurance.
- 11 B.Tech engineering branches (1,410 seats) and 8 M.Tech research specializations (162 seats).
- Industry-backed Centres of Excellence with Eicher Tractors, Netlink, UltraTech, and Texas Instruments.

## 4. Eligibility Criteria & Admission Process for Engineering Courses

### Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech - 4 Years) Eligibility & Process
- **Qualification**: 10+2 (Higher Secondary) with Physics and Mathematics as compulsory subjects plus Chemistry/Biotechnology/Biology/Technical Vocational subject.
- **Minimum Marks**: 45% aggregate for General category; 40% aggregate for SC/ST/OBC candidates of Madhya Pradesh.
- **Admission Path**: Merit in JEE Main scorecard and MP DTE Centralized Online Counseling / College Level Counseling (CLC).
- **B.Tech Lateral Entry (2nd Year)**: Passed 3-year Engineering Diploma with 45% marks (40% for reserved) or B.Sc. with Mathematics.

### Master of Technology (M.Tech - 2 Years) Eligibility & Process
- **Qualification**: Bachelor's Degree in Engineering/Technology (B.E./B.Tech) in relevant discipline from AICTE-recognized university.
- **Minimum Marks**: Minimum 50% marks or equivalent CGPA (45% for SC/ST/OBC candidates of MP).
- **Admission Path**: Valid GATE scorecard holders given first preference, followed by MP DTE merit counseling.

## 5. Technical Skill Roadmap & Pedagogy (Semester I to VIII)

### 360-Degree Skill Enhancement Framework
- **Semester I - II (Foundation)**: Refresher programming in C/C++, engineering graphics, basic electronics, and quantitative aptitude.
- **Semester III - IV (Core Tech)**: Object-Oriented Programming (Core Java), Data Structures & Algorithms (DSA), DBMS, and summer industrial internships.
- **Semester V - VI (Advanced Emerging Tech)**: Advanced Python, Machine Learning, Full-Stack Web Development, IoT & Cloud Computing, and placement-oriented coding drills.
- **Semester VII - VIII (Corporate Readiness)**: Company-specific technical training, competitive coding mock drives, corporate soft skills, and major industry capstone projects.
