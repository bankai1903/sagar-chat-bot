## 3A. On-Campus Residential Hostels & Room Amenities

### Boys & Girls Accommodation ('Good Food | Good Mood | Peace of Mind')
SISTec provides secure on-campus residential facilities across Gandhi Nagar and Ratibad:

- **Room Furnishing**: Individual bed with mattress, personal study desk, study chair, and lockable wardrobe.
