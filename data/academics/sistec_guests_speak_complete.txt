### SISTEC GUESTS' SPEAK - GOVERNOR SHRI MANGUBHAI C. PATEL (PART 2)
Speaker: Hon'ble Governor of Madhya Pradesh, Shri Mangubhai C. Patel
Event: Sagar Glory 2026 Placement Day (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RvJtn8_tEU4)

Key Pillars of the Governor's Address:
1. Honour Parents & Teachers: Always respect parents and educators who laid the foundation for your success.
2. Mother's Love is Irreplaceable: "Money comes and goes — but a mother's love and blessings are irreplaceable."
3. Uplift the Underprivileged: Use engineering knowledge and technological skills to solve grassroots challenges and empower the disadvantaged.
4. Hard Work & Integrity: Upright character, honesty, and continuous perseverance are the true hallmarks of lasting leadership.
5. Dedication to Duty as Patriotism: Drawing inspiration from PM Narendra Modi's work ethic, do your daily duty with full commitment.
6. Viksit Bharat @ 2047: Actively participate in transforming India into a developed, self-reliant global superpower.
