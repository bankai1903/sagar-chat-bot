## 1. Institutional Overview & Accreditations

### Overview
Sagar Institute of Science and Technology (SISTec), established in the year 2007, is the premier flagship engineering institute of the Sagar Group. Housed in a picturesque, lush green campus opposite Bhopal International Airport in Gandhi Nagar, SISTec has carved a niche as one of the best engineering colleges in Bhopal and Central India through empirical academic methodologies, state-of-the-art infrastructure, and strong campus placement records.

### Affiliations & Recognitions
- **University Affiliation**: Affiliated to **Rajiv Gandhi Proudyogiki Vishwavidyalaya (RGPV)**, Bhopal (State Technological University of Madhya Pradesh).
- **Statutory Approval**: Approved by the **All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE)**, New Delhi.
- **Accreditation**: **NAAC Accredited Institution** with multiple engineering programs accredited by the **National Board of Accreditation (NBA)** (including CSE, ME, CE, and ECE).

## 2. Key Academic & Innovation Pillars

### Empirical Academic Delivery & C2C Pedagogy
- Employs an interdisciplinary, practical approach combining university curriculum with specialized technical modules.
- Conducts structured semester-wise **Campus Recruitment Training (CRT)** and **Campus-to-Corporate (C2C)** modules focusing on aptitude, coding (Java, Python, C++, DSA), soft skills, and group discussion grooming from early semesters.

### Entrepreneurship Development Cell (EDC)
- Inculcates the spirit of entrepreneurship among technocrats.
- Nurtures student startup ideas from ideation to business prototyping, legal incorporation, and angel seed funding.

### Institution Innovation Council (IIC - MoE / MHRD)
- Selected by the **Ministry of Education / MHRD, Government of India**, to establish an on-campus **Institution Innovation Council (IIC)**.
- Organizes expert industry seminars, national webinars, design thinking workshops, and student hackathons.

## 3. Academic Programs Offered at SISTec (Gandhi Nagar)

### Undergraduate Programs (B.Tech - 4 Years)
- **Computer Science & Engineering (CSE)**: Core computing, software engineering, algorithms, and cloud systems.
- **Computer Science & Engineering - Artificial Intelligence & Data Science (CSE-AIDS)**: Machine learning, big data, deep learning, and predictive analytics (Headed by Dr. Vasima Khan).
- **Computer Science & Engineering - Cyber Security (CSE-CS)**: Network security, cryptography, digital forensics, and ethical hacking.
- **Electronics & Communication Engineering (ECE)**: Signal processing, communication systems, IoT, and embedded electronics.
- **Electrical & Electronics Engineering (EX)**: Power electronics, electrical drives, smart grid, and renewable energy.
- **Mechanical Engineering (ME)**: Robotics, CAD/CAM, automotive tech, thermodynamics, and manufacturing automation.
- **Civil Engineering (CE)**: Structural engineering, surveying, environmental engineering, and construction management.

### Postgraduate Programs (M.Tech - 2 Years)
- **Computer Science & Engineering (CSE)**
- **Thermal Power Engineering (Mechanical)**
- **Machine Design (Mechanical)**
- **Digital Communication (ECE)**
- **VLSI Design (Electronics)**
- **Power Systems (Electrical)**
- **Construction Technology & Management (Civil)**

### Management Programs (MBA - 2 Years)
- Conducted under **SISTec Business School** on the Gandhi Nagar campus with specializations in Marketing, Finance, Human Resource Management (HR), Banking & Financial Services, and Information Technology (IT).
