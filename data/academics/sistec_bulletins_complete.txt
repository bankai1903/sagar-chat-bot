# SAGAR GROUP OF INSTITUTIONS (SISTec) - OFFICIAL BULLETINS & NEWSLETTERS GUIDE

## 1A. Overview, Purpose & Publication Architecture
- **Official Bulletins Portal**: https://www.sistec.ac.in/bulletins-newsletters
- **Nature of Publication**:
  - The SISTec Bulletins are official monthly newsmagazines published by the Sagar Group of Institutions (SISTec).
  - Designed as comprehensive periodicals documenting academic excellence, technical breakthroughs, student achievements, campus life, and corporate relations across constituent colleges.
- **Dual Campus Publication Streams**:
  1. **The SISTec Bulletin**: Published for SISTec Gandhi Nagar campus, covering Engineering (SISTec), Pharmacy (SIPTec), and Management (SISTec Business School).
  2. **The SISTec-R Bulletin**: Published for SISTec Ratibad campus, covering SISTec-R and SISTec-E.
- **Publication Frequency**: Issued at the end of every calendar month (Monthly Periodical).

## 5A. Editorial Board, Student Journalism & Contribution Process
- **Central Editorial Governance**:
  - Overseen by a Central Editorial Board consisting of the Chief Editor (senior Professor of English & Communications), faculty editorial coordinators from each academic department, and graphic designers from the Digital Media Cell.
- **Student Activity Council (SAC) Participation**:
  - The SAC Digital and Photography Committee works hand-in-hand with the editorial board as student photojournalists, campus correspondents, and event reporters.
- **Submission & Contribution Guidelines**:
  - Faculty members and students can submit noteworthy achievements (conference papers, competition awards, poetry, original artwork, startup pitches, and technical articles) to their Departmental Bulletin Coordinator.
  - Submissions undergo editorial review, language polishing, and layout formatting before publication.

## 5B. Strategic Stakeholder Value & Accreditation Significance
The monthly SISTec Bulletins serve crucial institutional functions:
1. **Accreditation & Regulatory Documentation**:
   - Provides verified, time-stamped documentary evidence of institutional activities, co-curricular engagements, and research outputs for **NAAC, NBA, AICTE, and NIRF** ranking audits.
2. **Transparent Stakeholder Communication**:
   - Bridges the gap between the college, parents, and alumni, offering full visibility into academic schedules, student safety, and holistic student grooming.
3. **Employer & Recruiter Engagement**:
   - Shared with visiting corporate HR leaders and recruiters as a dynamic portfolio of student capabilities, hands-on learning, and institutional vitality.
