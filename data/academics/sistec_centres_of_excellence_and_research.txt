# SISTec Bhopal - Centres of Excellence (CoE), Research & Industry MoUs

## Centres of Excellence (CoE)
SISTec houses specialized industry-grade Centres of Excellence to train students beyond conventional academic curriculum:

1. **CoE in Semiconductor Technology**:
   - Focus: VLSI chip design, microelectronics, and semiconductor fabrication fundamentals.
   - Industry-Standard Platforms: Fully integrated with EDA (Electronic Design Automation) suites from **Synopsys** and **Cadence**—the exact industry tools powering global semiconductor design.
   - Host of the Madhya Pradesh Semiconductor Summit.

2. **SISTec-Eicher Centre of Excellence in Farm Machinery (Ratibad Campus)**:
   - Industry Partner: TMTL (Tractors and Motors Ltd.) - EICHER Tractors.
   - Purpose: Direct industry-institute partnership providing hands-on training on advanced agricultural tractors, precision farm implements, hydraulic systems, and automotive mechanics.

3. **CoE in Artificial Intelligence & Cloud Computing**:
   - Focus: Deep learning, neural networks, natural language processing, containerization, and cloud deployment architectures (AWS, Google Cloud, Microsoft Azure).
   - Practical work on high-performance GPU workstations.

4. **CoE in Data Analytics**:
   - Focus: Big Data frameworks, business intelligence (BI), predictive modeling, Tableau, PowerBI, and Python for statistical modeling.

5. **CoE in Internet of Things (IoT)**:
   - Focus: Embedded systems, sensor networks, microcontrollers (Arduino, ESP32, Raspberry Pi), edge computing, and smart home/industrial automation.

6. **UltraTech Centre of Excellence**:
   - Industry Partner: UltraTech Cement.
   - Focus: Advanced concrete technology, non-destructive civil testing, structural testing, and sustainable construction techniques.

7. **Advance Automotive Engineering CoE**:
   - Focus: Electric vehicles (EV), battery management systems (BMS), vehicle dynamics, and powertrain diagnostics.

## Research & Innovation Projects
Faculty and students actively build patented and applied engineering innovations:
- **Agricultural Drone for Targeted Crop Spraying**: Drone equipped with GPS guidance and machine vision cameras for automated precision pesticide and fertilizer delivery.
- **AI Account Management & Financial Literacy System**: Machine learning system for secure automated accounting and digital fraud awareness.
- **Solar Powered Tubular Fresh Water Still**: Renewable water purification system extracting potable water using solar thermal energy.
- **Pharmaceutical Drug Solubility Device (SIPTec)**: Patented mechanical device for enhancing solubility of poorly soluble active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs).
- **Ethical AI & Cybersecurity Defense Lab**: Research into automated threat detection and vulnerability assessments.

## Industry MoUs & Corporate Collaborations
SISTec maintains active Memorandums of Understanding (MoUs) with leading corporations for internships, certified curricula, and lab setups:
- Red Hat Academy, AWS Academy, Cisco Networking Academy, Microsoft Learn, Salesforce Trailhead Ecosystem, Eicher Tractors (TMTL), UltraTech Cement, and Texas Instruments.
