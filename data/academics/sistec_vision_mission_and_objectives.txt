# SAGAR INSTITUTE OF SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY (SISTec) - VISION, MISSION & OBJECTIVES

## Source & Navigation Context
- **Official URL**: https://www.sistec.ac.in/sgi-vision-mission
- **Navigation Path**: Main Website -> "ABOUT US" -> Sub-menu Item 2: "Vision & Mission"
- **Parent Welfare Society**: Shri Agrawal Educational & Welfare Society (Established 2007)
- **Governing Group**: Sagar Group of Institutions (SGI), Bhopal

## 1. Institutional Objective
**Official Statement**:
> "To become a One-stop Solution for students in order to build their successful career."

**Significance & Implementation**:
- Provides a comprehensive, 360-degree educational ecosystem integrating foundational academics, hands-on lab work, skill-building, personality development, and industry placement under one roof.
- Ensures every student transitions seamlessly from college to a flourishing corporate, higher education, or entrepreneurial career.

## 2. Institutional Vision
**Official Statement**:
> "To become an institution of excellence in Science & Technology, by imparting world-class quality education and relevant training; producing skilled, competent & innovative workforce having social and ethical concern towards nation building."

**Core Vision Dimensions**:
- **Excellence in Science & Technology**: Benchmarking pedagogical standards against premier global engineering and tech institutes.
- **World-Class Quality & Relevant Training**: Combining rigorous academic curricula with practical laboratory and emerging-tech training modules.
- **Skilled, Competent & Innovative Workforce**: Nurturing technical agility, problem-solving skills, and startup thinking.
- **Social & Ethical Commitment**: Instilling integrity, professional ethics, and active contribution to nation building.

## 3. Institutional Mission Statements (The 5 Mission Pillars)
SISTec accomplishes its institutional vision through five fundamental student-centric mission pillars:

1. **Pillar 1 - Academic Ecosystem & Lifelong Learning**:
   "To establish a student-centric academic ecosystem enabling them to acquire & retain domain knowledge, develop lifelong learning attitude and maintain professional ethics."
2. **Pillar 2 - Industry-Relevant Skills & Grooming**:
   "To induce career-oriented, industry-relevant skills in students, by means of appropriate & updated training modules for their overall grooming."
3. **Pillar 3 - State-of-the-Art Infrastructure & Innovation**:
   "To provide state of art facilities and infrastructure to facilitate seamless dissemination of technical knowledge and spirit of innovation among students."
4. **Pillar 4 - Industrial Tie-ups, Research & Societal Service**:
   "To foster collaborative development through industrial tie-ups and research, so that the students can serve the society."
5. **Pillar 5 - Faculty Development & Knowledge Transmission**:
   "To enable continuous development of teachers, thereby ensuring updated knowledge transmission to our students."

### Implementation Details of the 5 Mission Pillars

- **Pillar 1 (Academic Ecosystem)**:
  Focuses on participatory classrooms, tutorial sessions, remedial classes, domain knowledge retention, lifelong self-learning, and professional ethics.

- **Pillar 2 (Industry Skills & C2C Training)**:
  Structured semester-wise Campus-to-Corporate (C2C) training from 1st to 8th semester, aptitude tests, group discussions, and technical bootcamps (AI/ML, Python, Java, Cloud).

- **Pillar 3 (Infrastructure & Innovation)**:
  High-speed Wi-Fi campus, modern auditoriums, seven advanced departmental laboratories, 3D printing labs, and Centres of Excellence (VLSI, IoT, AI, Eicher, Concrete).

- **Pillar 4 (Industrial Tie-ups & Research)**:
  50+ active industrial MoUs, government-funded research projects (DST, MPCST), rural technology outreach, and student innovation events (Nirman, Mini-Anveshana).

- **Pillar 5 (Faculty Development Programs - FDPs)**:
  Mandatory Faculty Development Programs (FDPs), industry immersion for teachers, research publication incentives, and support for doctoral (Ph.D.) research.

## 4. Why Choose Sagar Group Colleges? (The 10 Core Tenets - "The 10 We's")
Sagar Group Colleges articulate institutional commitment through ten core operational tenets ("The 10 We's"):
- **1. We Stand**: Committed to quality Engineering, Pharmacy, and Management education.
- **2. We Understand**: Each student's needs, placing them on track via Counseling & Mentoring.
- **3. We Care**: Quality education via evolving pedagogy and 25 years rich experience.
- **4. We Execute**: Best teaching-learning practices with highly qualified, skilled faculty.
- **5. We Groom**: Structured training and industry-aligned skills for Magnificent Placements.
- **6. We Value**: Quality while attaining all Program Outcomes (POs) and student soft skills.
- **7. We Build**: Transparent, student-centric innovation and startup ecosystem.
- **8. We Infuse**: Social, cultural, and ethical values in our students.
- **9. We Improvise**: Academic curriculum dynamically aligned with changing industry demands.
- **10. We Excel**: In all student success sectors - Placement, Higher Education, and Entrepreneurship.
