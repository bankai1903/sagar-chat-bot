## 1. Institutional Overview & Management Philosophy of SISTec Business School

### Institutional Overview
Sagar Institute of Science and Technology - School of Management Studies, popularly known as **SISTec Business School (SISTec MBA)**, was established in the year 2009 under the visionary leadership of Shri Sudhir Kumar Agrawal (Chairman, Sagar Group). Recognized as one of the top NAAC Accredited B-Schools and management colleges in Bhopal, Madhya Pradesh, SISTec Business School is committed to delivering world-class business leadership education.

### Management Philosophy & Core Values
- Operates under the defining institutional philosophy: **'Business in Mind with Humanity at Heart'**.
- Blends academic rigor with corporate acumen, ethical leadership, strategic thinking, and corporate social sensitivity.
- Faculty team comprises experienced academics and seasoned corporate practitioners who impart practical case studies, live business intelligence, and real-world executive problem solving.

### Affiliations, Approvals & Accreditations
- **Statutory Technical Approval**: Approved by the **All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE)**, New Delhi.
- **University Affiliation**: Affiliated to **Barkatullah Vishwavidyalaya (Barkatullah University - BU Bhopal)**.
- **Accreditation**: **NAAC Accredited Institution**.
- **National Recognition**: Named among **"The 10 Best Institutes for Finance Studies in India, 2018"** by *The Knowledge Review Magazine*.

## 3. Academic Programs & Degrees Offered at SISTec Business School

### Master of Business Administration (MBA - 2 Years / 4 Semesters)
- **Degree Awarded**: Master of Business Administration (MBA), affiliated to Barkatullah University (BU Bhopal) and approved by AICTE.
- **Annual Intake**: 120 Seats.
- **Eligibility**: Bachelor's degree in any discipline (Commerce, Science, Engineering, or Humanities) from a recognized university.
- **Dual Specialization Concept**: Students choose two major functional specializations out of five electives:
  1. **Financial Management (Finance)**
  2. **Marketing Management (Marketing)**
  3. **Human Resource Management (HRM)**
  4. **Retail Management**
  5. **Banking & Financial Services Management (BFSI)**

### Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA - 3 Years / 6 Semesters)
- **Degree Awarded**: Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA), affiliated to Barkatullah University (BU Bhopal).
- Focuses on business fundamentals, managerial economics, accounting, organizational behavior, marketing principles, and entrepreneurship.

## 4. MBA Curriculum Structure & Semester Breakdown (BU Bhopal)

### Core Curriculum Roadmap
- **Semester I**: Management Concepts & Practices (CP-101), Quantitative Methods (CP-102), Managerial Economics (CP-103), Communication Skills (CP-104), International Business Environment (CP-105), Accounting for Managers (CP-106).
- **Semester II**: Organizational Behaviour (CP-201), Human Resource Management (CP-202), Financial Management (CP-203), Marketing Management (CP-204), Research Methodology (CP-205), Operations Management (CP-206).
- **Semester III**: Business Ethics & Legal Environment, Strategic Management, Summer Internship Project (SIP), Specialization Electives (Major I & Major II - 4 Papers).
- **Semester IV**: Entrepreneurship Development, Major Research Project, Comprehensive Viva-Voce, Specialization Electives (Major I & Major II - 4 Papers).

## 6. Student Councils, Signature Events & Campus Life at SISTec Business School

### Student Leadership Bodies & Signature Forums
