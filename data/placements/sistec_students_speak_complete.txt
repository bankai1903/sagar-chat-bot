[SISTEC STUDENTS SPEAK - VIDEO GALLERY OVERVIEW & TESTIMONIALS]
The 'Students' Speak' section of the SISTec Video Gallery (https://www.sistec.ac.in/video-gallery) showcases 43 candid video testimonials and placement bytes from graduating scholars across Batches 2025, 2024, and 2023.
- Core Themes Highlighted:
  * Campus-to-Corporate (C2C) Training: Structured modules covering quantitative aptitude, soft skills, mock interviews, and competitive coding.
  * Faculty Mentorship: Supportive professors, approachable HODs, and personalized semester project guidance.
  * Advanced Infrastructure: Practical exposure in Centres of Excellence (Eicher, Renault Nissan, Gaia IoT), 100-computer HPC cluster, and robotics bays.
  * Placement Cell Support: Regular on-campus recruitment drives by Fortune 500 multinationals (TCS, Infosys, Cognizant, Cisco, Netlink, HCLTech, Capgemini, BNY Mellon).
- Video Formats: Short video bytes ('Placement Bytes' and 'Student Spotlight') accessible on YouTube and the SISTec Mobile App.
