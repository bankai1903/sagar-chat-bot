## 9. Campus to Corporate (C2C) Training Modules & Assessments

### Semester-Wise Employability Roadmap
- **Semester 1 & 2**: Foundational communication, business vocabulary, public speaking, personality grooming, and logical thinking.
- **Semester 3 & 4**: Quantitative aptitude fundamentals, data interpretation, core coding languages (C, C++, Java, Python), and database management.
- **Semester 5 & 6**: Advanced algorithms, Data Structures (DSA), full-stack development, cloud computing, company-specific aptitude tracks, and resume crafting.
- **Semester 7 & 8**: Intensive mock recruitment drives, Technical and HR mock interviews, group discussions, and live campus hiring drives.
- **National Benchmark Assessments**: AMCAT and CoCubes tests administered up to 8 times over 4 years to monitor candidate percentile standings nationally.
