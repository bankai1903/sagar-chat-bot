# SISTec Bhopal - Training, Corporate Relations & Placements (T&P)

## Training & Placement Cell Overview
The Training & Placement (T&P) Cell at Sagar Institute of Science and Technology (SISTec) operates with a dedicated mission of making students corporate-ready from the second year itself through rigorous technical, aptitude, coding, and soft-skill development programs.

## Placement Key Highlights & Statistics
- **Placement Rate**: Consistently achieves an average of ~96% placement record across eligible students during major placement drives.
- **Job Offers**: Over 1,150+ job offers generated across campuses annually.
- **Highest Salary Package**: Up to ₹21 LPA - ₹22 LPA offered by premium tech firms (e.g. Cisco, Infosys Power Programmer, etc.).
- **Average Salary Package**: Ranges between ₹4.5 LPA to ₹4.8 LPA across engineering disciplines.
- **Participating Companies**: 150+ national and multinational corporate partners visit for on-campus and pool-campus hiring every year.

## Student Development & Pre-Placement Training
- **Campus to Corporate (C2C) Modules**: Structured 360-degree training encompassing quantitative aptitude, logical reasoning, verbal ability, and group discussion etiquette.
- **Coding & Technical Bootcamps**: Intensive hands-on training in Data Structures & Algorithms (DSA), Full-Stack Web Development, Python, Java, Cloud Computing, and AI/ML.
- **Mock Interviews**: One-on-one technical and HR mock interviews conducted by corporate HR veterans and senior alumni.
- **Summer Internships**: Mandatory 4 to 8-week internships facilitated across prominent industries and research institutions between 4th and 7th semesters.

## T&P Cell Eligibility Guidelines for Campus Drives
- Students typically require an aggregate CGPA of 6.0 or 6.5+ without active backlogs to be eligible for tier-1 IT and core campus drives.
- Minimum 75% attendance in mandatory training modules and coding assessments is required to receive placement assistance.
- "One Student One Job" standard policy applies, with special dream job / super-dream options allowed if the subsequent offer package is substantially higher (usually 1.5x to 2x).
