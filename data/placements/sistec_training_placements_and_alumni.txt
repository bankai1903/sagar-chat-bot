About Training and Placement
The Training and Placement Department (T&P) at SISTec is dedicated to empowering students to achieve their career aspirations through structured guidance, industry exposure, and continuous support. The T&P fosters a culture of lifelong learning, enabling students to build the skills, confidence, and adaptability required to thrive in an evolving job market. Starting from the first year, the T&P ensures that students are consistently guided through every stage of their career journey—from exploration to placement and beyond.
## Our Approach
T&P adopts a holistic and technology-driven approach to career readiness, combining personalised mentorship with advanced tools and practical exposure.
* Early career orientation beginning in the first year
* AI-enabled training app, Pod AI, for skill development and performance tracking
* Dedicated placement-focused events like SISTec Headstart, bridging industry and academia
* Training events like Number Games, Communication Premier League, and Sagar Setu to improve interview readiness
* Recruitment drives with leading organisations such as TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Accenture, Cognizant, and Deloitte
* Continuous placement guidance and career counselling
* Access to experienced mentors and industry professionals
* Special initiatives like Sagar Glory to recognise and celebrate student achievements
## Key Objectives
* To help students explore career paths and make informed decisions through the right tools and guidance
* To equip students with industry-relevant skills and competencies
* To build strong connections with recruiters and create diverse job opportunities
* To support students throughout their placement journey and provide ongoing career guidance

Alumni Meet
SISTec Alumni Meet 2024
Date: 28-12-2024
Sagar Group of Institutions-SISTec successfully hosted Confluentia 2024, a vibrant reunion that brought together alumni from engineering, pharmacy, and management streams to celebrate "Memories and Moments." The event kicked off at the Vikram Auditorium with a nostalgic video collage of past meets, followed by high-energy sports and games that rekindled the competitive spirit of hostel days. The atmosphere was further enriched by the presence of alumni from the Gandhi Nagar campus and esteemed ex-faculty members, creating a profound sense of community. Current students welcomed their seniors with captivating musical performances and dynamic dance mash-ups, while alumni and former professors shared invaluable insights and guidance, bridging the gap between cherished traditions and future aspirations.
A poignant segment of the afternoon was dedicated to honoring our distinguished ex-faculty and outstanding alumni with mementos and gifts, recognizing the lasting impact they have made on the institution. The celebration reached its crescendo with a soulful musical performance by The Roots Band, featuring lead singer and Civil Engineering alumnus Mr. Anirudh Saxena (Batch 2014).
SISTec Alumni Meet 2023
Date: 23-12-2023
Sagar Group of Institutions - SISTec Colleges conducted Alumni Meet - Confluentia 2023 with great zeal and enthusiasm. The event was full of nostalgia and expressions of thoughts. It was a great experience to share screens with Alumni from all 5 colleges coming up together as a single unit with a great bond. Confluentia 2023 witnessed the presence of over 150+ engineering, pharmacy, and management students re-uniting to cheer the glorious moments of their past and remember their good old days. Indulging selfie points at various popular locations with signature boards along with a musical and dance welcome performance by the current batch, got the students together.
The alumni shared their experiences and acknowledged learning experiences at SISTec as the foundation of their success. A few alumni also shared details about their startups which they conceptualized during SISTec Startup Meet. Clicking selfies at every corner of campus and making videos filling their mobiles with no space were the takeaways as lifetime recollections by the students. They enjoyed grooving to the beats of music followed by a very delightful and scrumptious lunch. Proud Alumni jackets were presented along with badges as mementos by the institution.
