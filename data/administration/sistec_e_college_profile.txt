## 1. Institutional Overview & Campus Identity of SISTec-E (Ratibad)

### Institutional Overview
Sagar Institute of Science, Technology and Engineering (SISTec-E) was established in 2009 under the visionary leadership of Shri Sudhir Kumar Agrawal (Chairman, Sagar Group) through Shri Agrawal Educational & Welfare Society. Situated in the serene suburbs of Bhopal amidst a sprawling 12-acre campus in Sikandrabad, Ratibad, SISTec-E has established itself as one of the premier private engineering colleges in Madhya Pradesh.

### Core Philosophy & Educational Vision
- Focuses on industry-oriented curriculum dissemination, modern pedagogical practices, and hands-on skill development that goes beyond conventional university syllabi.
- With engineering at its core, SISTec-E intends to contribute to modern society by serving as an influencing driver in technical innovation, sustainable energy, and digital transformation.
- Ultra-modern amenities blended with competitive academic consistency, strong industrial exposure, and state-of-the-art laboratory infrastructure.

### Affiliations, Approvals & Accreditations of SISTec-E
- **University Affiliation**: Affiliated to **Rajiv Gandhi Proudyogiki Vishwavidyalaya (RGPV)**, Bhopal (MP State Technical University).
- **Statutory Approval**: Approved by the **All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE)**, New Delhi.
- **Accreditation**: **NAAC Accredited Institution**.
- **Institutional Rating**: Rated **AAA** by **Careers360 Magazine** (consistently ranked among India's Best Engineering Institutes).

## 2. Research Milestones, TEQIP-III & Centres of Excellence at SISTec-E

### Major Sponsored Research Milestone (TEQIP-III / NPIU)
- SISTec-E successfully executed and completed a prestigious government-sponsored research project entitled **"Solar PV Training & Research Standalone System"**.
- This landmark project was conducted under the **Collaborative Research Scheme (CRS)** of the **Technical Education Quality Improvement Programme (TEQIP-III)**, sponsored by the **National Project Implementation Unit (NPIU)**, Ministry of Education, Government of India.
- The standalone solar PV facility serves as a live research platform for solar irradiance testing, PV module efficiency benchmarking, and green energy power generation.

### Industry-Backed Centres of Excellence & Innovation Infrastructure
- **Eicher Farm Machinery Centre of Excellence**: Collaborative hands-on training hub established with **Eicher Tractors (TMTL)** for agricultural machinery mechanics, tractor transmission, hydraulics, and automotive systems.
- **Netlink Centre of Excellence in Data Analytics**: Backed by Netlink for enterprise business intelligence, big data analytics, and cloud engineering.
- **Institutions Innovation Council (IIC)**: Active cell under MoE's Innovation Cell for fostering student patents, innovations, and startup prototypes.
- **Drone Club & Aviation Lab**: Dedicated student club conducting hands-on workshops in UAV drone design, aeromodelling, flight controllers, and autonomous navigation.
- **SPIC MACAY Chapter**: Active student wing organizing cultural heritage symposiums, classical arts, and holistic student enrichment.

## 3. Academic Programs & B.Tech / M.Tech Degrees Offered at SISTec-E

### Undergraduate Engineering Programs (B.Tech Branches at SISTec-E Ratibad - 4 Years)
SISTec-E offers full-time 4-year Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) degree programs affiliated to RGPV Bhopal:
- **Computer Science & Engineering (CSE)**: Core computing, algorithms, full-stack software development, software engineering, and cloud platforms.
- **CSE with Internet of Things (CSE - IoT)**: Embedded systems, sensor networks, microcontroller programming, IoT communication protocols, and edge analytics.
- **Electrical Engineering (EE)**: Electrical machines, power generation, control systems, switchgears, and renewable solar/wind energy grids.
- **Electronics & Communication Engineering (ECE)**: Digital signal processing, VLSI design, wireless communications, embedded microcontrollers, and RF systems.
- **Mechanical Engineering (ME)**: Thermodynamics, CAD/CAM, fluid mechanics, CNC machining, robotics, and automotive design.

### Postgraduate Engineering Programs (M.Tech at SISTec-E Ratibad - 2 Years)
- **M.Tech in Power Systems (Electrical Engineering)**: Advanced research-focused 2-year postgraduate program covering high-voltage power transmission, smart grid technology, power system protection, renewable energy integration, and computer-aided power analysis.
