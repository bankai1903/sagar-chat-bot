## 1. Institutional Overview & Research Identity of SISTec-R (Ratibad)

### Institutional Overview
Sagar Institute of Science, Technology and Research (SISTec-R), established in the year 2009, is situated amidst a picturesque, scenic natural environment with a sprawling campus in Ratibad, Bhopal. Recognized as one of the Top 10 Engineering Colleges in Bhopal, SISTec-R has distinguished itself as a premier hub of technical education, applied research, and innovation in Central India.

### Core Research Philosophy at SISTec-R Ratibad
- Operates with a revolutionizing, research-driven approach to nurture students into both skilled technocrats and creative researchers.
- Aims to transform into an R&D conglomerate in Central India, bridging academic research with industrial needs.

### Affiliations & Recognitions of SISTec-R
- **University Affiliation**: Affiliated to **Rajiv Gandhi Proudyogiki Vishwavidyalaya (RGPV)**, Bhopal (MP State Technical University).
- **Statutory Approval**: Approved by the **All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE)**, New Delhi.
- **Accreditation**: **NAAC Accredited Institution**.

## 2. Centres of Excellence & Innovation Infrastructure at SISTec-R (Ratibad)

### Eicher Farm Machinery Centre of Excellence at SISTec-R
- SISTec-R Ratibad features a dedicated **Eicher Farm Machinery Centre of Excellence**, established in collaborative partnership with **Eicher Tractors**.
- The Centre of Excellence provides intensive practical hands-on training and research in agricultural machinery, engine mechanics, tractor assembly, transmissions, hydraulic systems, and heavy automotive systems.
- Serves as a specialized research hub for Mechanical Engineering (ME) and automotive engineering students at SISTec-R.

### Institutions Innovation Council (IIC) at SISTec-R Ratibad
- Established on-campus under the Ministry of Education's (MoE) MHRD Innovation Cell.
- Consistently awarded a high **4-Star IIC Rating** for conducting expert talks, innovation seminars, patent workshops, prototype development, and technology transfer sessions.

### Renewable Energy & Green Technology Lab at SISTec-R Ratibad
- Focuses on solar energy innovations, agro-waste engineering, renewable power generation, and environmental sustainability initiatives.
