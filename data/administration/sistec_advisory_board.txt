# SAGAR INSTITUTE OF SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY (SISTec) - ADVISORY BOARD

## Source & Navigation Context
- **Official URL**: https://www.sistec.ac.in/advisory-board
- **Navigation Location**: Main Website -> "ABOUT US" -> Sub-menu Item 4: "Advisory Board"
- **Governing Body**: Sagar Group of Institutions (SGI) / Shri Agrawal Educational & Welfare Society (Established 2007)
- **Location**: Bhopal, Madhya Pradesh (Gandhi Nagar Campus & Ratibad Campus)

### Institutional Role of the Advisory Board
> *"Our advisory board brings together a diverse group of experienced professionals to provide strategic guidance and expertise for the advancement of our institution's goals."*
- The Advisory Board at SISTec consists of distinguished industry titans, research scientists, cloud architects, technical leads, corporate CXOs, and startup founders from leading global enterprises (Google, Microsoft, Intel, ISRO, TCS, Capgemini, Salesforce, Deloitte, Chalo, Gaia Smart Cities, and SMPL).
- They provide curriculum feedback, bridge academia-industry alignment, guide emerging technology initiatives (AI/ML, Cloud, VLSI, IoT), and mentor student career trajectories.
