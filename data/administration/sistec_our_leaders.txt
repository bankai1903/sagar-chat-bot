## 1. Executive Leadership & Founders

### Shri Sudhir Kumar Agrawal - Founder & Chairman, Sagar Group
- **Official Designation**: Founder & Chairman, Sagar Group (SGI)
- **Official Profile Photo**: `https://www.sistec.ac.in/assets/web/images/leaders/2024/sudhir-kumar-agrawal.webp`

- **Guiding Quote**:
  > *"We cannot always build the future for our youth, but we can build our youth for the future."* — Franklin D. Roosevelt

- **Chairman's Official Message & Educational Philosophy**:
  "Sagar Institute of Science and Technology® (SISTec) truly believes in these words by Franklin D. Roosevelt & it delineates our vision. We aspire to upskill students to LEARN, not just STUDY. Ergo we strive to endeavour beyond the boundaries of mere books. We have realised that the future is notional and unknown but the youth in our hands are real and can be sculpted. We are enabling our students to learn and master engineering and other technical skills with the best of teaching, training and learning methodologies and facilities. At SISTec we are committed to carve and shape the best human resource."

- **Institutional Impact**:
  Founded Sagar Group in 1983; expanded into education (SISTec colleges & Sagar Public Schools), healthcare (Sagar Multispeciality Hospital), industry (Sagar Manufacturers - SMPL spinning mill & Sagar Nutriments rice milling), and infrastructure (Agrawal Builders).

### Er. Siddharth Sudhir Agrawal - Managing Director, Sagar Group
- **Official Designation**: Managing Director, Sagar Group (SGI)
- **Official Profile Photo**: `https://www.sistec.ac.in/assets/web/images/leaders/2024/siddharth-sudhir-agrawal.webp`

- **Managing Director's Official Message & Entrepreneurship Vision**:
  "At Sagar Group of Institutions® (SISTec) we do not simply graduate the students, we also make them understand and learn different life skills. We make every possible attempt to mould our students in a way that they come out as strong individuals who can sustain in competitive world. Our extra curriculum programs combined with academic syllabus makes sure that what we profess is achieved for our students. We do not feed the students with mere facts and knowledge instead; we motivate our students to be the source of knowledge themselves. We also believe that students are our partners in our mission to make them entrepreneurs. Our main job is not only to get them jobs instead; facilitate them to provide jobs."

- **Key Focus Areas**:
  Life skills development, student entrepreneurship, industry bootcamps, and holistic student personality grooming.
