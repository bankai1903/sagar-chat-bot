## 2. Four Decades Historical Timeline & Milestones
- **1983 (The Genesis in Real Estate)**:
  - Shri Sudhir Kumar Agrawal founded the group with a humble single-room enterprise under the brand **Agrawal Builders**.
  - Built on integrity, quality materials, and punctual delivery, laying the foundation of trust across Bhopal.
- **2001 (Pioneering School Education - SPS)**:
  - Ventured into quality K-12 schooling with the establishment of **Sagar Public School (SPS) at Saket Nagar, Bhopal**.
  - Revolutionized school education in MP through state-of-the-art sports facilities, labs, and holistic cultural grooming.
- **2007 (Higher Technical Education - SISTec Inception)**:
  - Established the **Shri Agrawal Educational & Welfare Society** and launched **Sagar Institute of Science and Technology (SISTec) at Gandhi Nagar, Bhopal**.
  - Aimed at delivering industry-relevant engineering, pharmacy, and managerial education with focus on Campus-to-Corporate (C2C) readiness.
- **2009 - 2013 (Expansion of Technical Campuses & Sisters)**:
  - Expanded engineering education with **SISTec-R (Ratibad Campus)** in 2009.
  - Launched **SIPTec (Sagar Institute of Pharmacy & Technology)** and **SISTec-E (Engineering)**.
- **2012 (Venturing into Global Textiles - SMPL)**:
  - Established **Sagar Manufacturers Pvt. Ltd. (SMPL)** at Tamot, Goharganj, near Bhopal.
  - Created a globally competitive spinning mill with world-class automation and launched the **"Sagar Yarn"** brand.
- **2014 (Institutionalizing Philanthropy - Abhay Sagar Foundation)**:
  - Formalized CSR through **Abhay Sagar Foundation** and launched **Sagar Vidya Niketan (SVN)** to provide 100% free English-medium education, meals, and books to underprivileged children.
- **2016 (Agribusiness & Food Processing - Sagar Rice)**:
  - Set up **Sagar Nutriments Pvt. Ltd. (SNPL)**, setting up Central India's most advanced, fully automated paddy milling unit for premium Basmati and Non-Basmati rice.
- **2021 - Present (Healthcare Revolution & Sustainable Growth)**:
  - Commissioned **Sagar Multispeciality Hospital (SMH)**, bringing tertiary care, advanced cardiac, neuro, oncology, and emergency healthcare to Bhopal.
  - SPS school chain expanded to 6 active campuses, with the 7th campus **SPS Misrod** scheduled for 2027.

## 11. Environmental Sustainability & Green Governance
- **Carbon Offsetting**: The group has implemented environmental regenerative practices equivalent to planting over **2,00,000 (2 Lakh) trees**.
- **Solar Energy Generation**: Multi-megawatt rooftop solar photovoltaic installations across SISTec campuses, SPS schools, and SMPL spinning mills, generating millions of clean green electricity units annually.
- **Zero Liquid Discharge (ZLD) & Water Stewardship**:
  - Full-capacity Sewage Treatment Plants (STP) recycle 100% of wastewater for lush campus greenery and agricultural landscaping.
  - Rainwater harvesting systems across all educational campuses and residential townships recharge local groundwater aquifers.
