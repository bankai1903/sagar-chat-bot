## Student Support Cells & Grievance Redressal
1. **Internal Complaints Committee (ICC)**: Ensures gender sensitization and addresses complaints regarding harassment as per UGC/AICTE norms.
2. **Anti-Ragging Committee & Flying Squad**:
   - Zero tolerance policy towards ragging.
   - 24/7 National Anti-Ragging Helpline: 1800-180-5522.
   - College Anti-Ragging Squad phone numbers are prominently displayed across academic blocks and hostels.
3. **Student Grievance Redressal Cell (SGRC)**:
   - Online portal and physical suggestion/complaint boxes provided for student concerns regarding academics, examinations, or facilities.
   - Time-bound resolution within 7 working days.
4. **SC / ST / OBC & Minority Welfare Cell**:
   - Assists students with state and national government scholarship applications and addresses reservation-related matters.
5. **Women Empowerment Cell (WEC)**:
   - Organizes workshops, self-defense sessions, health checkups, and empowerment seminars for female students and staff.
