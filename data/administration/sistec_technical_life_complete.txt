## 2. Signature National Competitions & Hackathons

### Premier Technical Competitions
- **Nirmaan (Annual Working Model Competition)**:
  - National-level project exhibition where engineering and pharmacy students showcase working hardware prototypes, IoT systems, robotics, and societal innovations judged by industry leaders.
- **Quad Torc (National Student Off-Road Championship)**:
  - Inter-collegiate all-terrain vehicle (ATV) and quad bike design, fabrication, and off-road racing championship organized under the SAE India student chapter.
- **SISTec Innovation Hackathon (SIH 2.0)**:
  - 24-hour non-stop national software and hardware hackathon. Over 70 teams comprising 350+ students build innovative solutions for governance, industry, and social welfare under CSI auspices.
- **BAJA SAE India & SUPRA Racing**:
  - Collegiate teams engineer formula race cars and all-terrain buggies for prestigious national motorsport contests.

## 3. IEEE Student Branch & CSI Student Chapter

### Computing, AI & Electrical Engineering Forums
- **IEEE Student Branch (Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers)**:
  - Organizes advanced workshops like 'Generative AI & Agentic AI: From Foundations to Intelligent Applications' covering LLMs, Prompt Engineering, RAG, vector databases, and autonomous AI agents.
  - Specialized industry sessions including SMPS Circuit Design for LEDs and IEEE Day celebrations graced by IEEE MP Section chairs and BHEL senior leadership.
- **Computer Society of India (CSI) Student Chapter**:
  - Organizes practical technology bootcamps such as 'From Sensors to Intelligence: IoT Meets GenAI on Cloud' (sensors, cloud communication, and generative AI).
  - Hosts annual coding sprints, algorithmic hackathons, and web application competitions.

## 4. SAE India Collegiate Club & Institution's Innovation Council (IIC)

### Automotive Engineering & Innovation Ecosystem
- **Society of Automotive Engineers (SAE India) Collegiate Club**:
  - Hands-on fabrication hub for mechanical, electrical, and automotive students building off-road ATVs, e-vehicles, and formula student racers.
  - Consistent podium finishes at national Quad Torc and BAJA events.
- **Institution's Innovation Council (IIC)**:
  - Established under the Ministry of Education (MoE) Innovation Cell.
  - Organizes 'World Creativity & Innovation Day' with signature categories: 'Junk Genius' (hardware innovation from scraps) and 'Code and Craft' (software apps).
  - Conducts Intellectual Property Rights (IPR) and patent filing workshops sponsored by the MP Council of Science and Technology (MPCST Bhopal).

## 5. Solar Energy Society (SESI) & BIS Standards Club

### Renewable Energy & Quality Standardization
- **Solar Energy Society of India (SESI) Student Chapter**:
  - Promotes clean energy research, rooftop solar PV installations, and energy conservation.
  - Conducts technical masterclasses with global leaders like Hitachi Energy Sweden on High Voltage Direct Current (HVDC) systems (HVDC LCC and VSC).
  - Observes National Energy Conservation Day under the theme 'Conserve to Preserve'.
- **Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) Standards Club**:
  - Promotes standardization, product testing, system verification, and quality compliance among students.
  - Organizes Standard Writing Competitions (e.g., pressure cooker safety standards) and quality control quizzes with cash awards.

## 6. ISTE, IETE, IEI & NPTEL Local Chapters

### Professional Dissemination & Certifications
- **Indian Society for Technical Education (ISTE)**:
  - Organizes the National Startup Meet and ISTE Annual Convention MP Section connecting young innovators with Bhopal Smart City and startup founders.
  - Conducts 'Ink & Innovation' technical and creative poetry symposiums.
- **The Institution of Electronics and Telecommunication Engineers (IETE)**:
  - Organizes World Telecommunication Day workshops in collaboration with Department of Telecommunications (DoT) on 5G lab vision, cybersecurity, and cyber fraud prevention.
- **The Institution of Engineers (India) (IEI)**:
  - Conducts real-world engineering site visits like the Indore-Budni New Railway Line NATM Tunnel construction and IoT applications in civil structures.
- **NPTEL Local Chapter**:
  - Mentors students for online IIT MOOC certifications in DSA, Python, Java, IoT, and Power Plant Engineering.
