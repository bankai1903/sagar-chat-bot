## 1. Institutional Overview & Campus Identity of SIPTec (Gandhi Nagar)

### Institutional Overview
Sagar Institute of Pharmacy and Technology (SIPTec) was established in 2008 under the aegis of Shri Agrawal Educational & Welfare Society, founded by Shri Sudhir Kumar Agrawal (Chairman, Sagar Group). Situated at the flagship Gandhi Nagar campus opposite Raja Bhoj International Airport in Bhopal, SIPTec has emerged as one of the premier pharmacy institutions in Central India.

### Core Philosophy & Healthcare Mission
- SIPTec is committed to preparing highly skilled pharmacy graduates who can fulfill the public need for safe, ethical, and effective pharmaceutical care.
- The institute integrates standard university curriculum with advanced industrial formulation modules, drug design, regulatory affairs, clinical trials, and pharmacology practices.
- Emphasizes basic and applied pharmaceutical research, community healthcare awareness, and cutting-edge laboratory training.

### Affiliations, Approvals & Accreditations of SIPTec
- **Statutory Council Approval**: Approved by the **Pharmacy Council of India (PCI)**, New Delhi.
- **Technical Approval**: Approved by the **All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE)**, New Delhi.
- **University Affiliation**: Affiliated to **Rajiv Gandhi Proudyogiki Vishwavidyalaya (RGPV)**, Bhopal (MP State Technical University).
- **Accreditation**: **NAAC Accredited Institution**.

## 3. Academic Programs & Pharmacy Degrees Offered at SIPTec

### Undergraduate Pharmacy Program (B.Pharm - 4 Years / 8 Semesters)
- **Bachelor of Pharmacy (B.Pharm)**:
  - 4-year full-time professional degree program approved by PCI and AICTE, affiliated to RGPV Bhopal.
  - Comprehensive curriculum covering Human Anatomy & Physiology, Pharmaceutical Chemistry, Pharmaceutics, Pharmacognosy, Pharmacology, Pharmaceutical Analysis, Biopharmaceutics, Jurisprudence, and Industrial Formulation.
  - Includes mandatory industrial training, hospital pharmacy internships, and project work.

### Postgraduate Pharmacy Programs (M.Pharm - 2 Years / 4 Semesters)
SIPTec offers full-time 2-year Master of Pharmacy (M.Pharm) degrees affiliated to RGPV in two key specializations:
- **M.Pharm in Pharmaceutics**: Focuses on advanced novel drug delivery systems (NDDS), dosage form design, nanomedicines, biopharmaceutics, pharmacokinetic modeling, and industrial scale-up.
- **M.Pharm in Pharmaceutical Chemistry**: Advanced organic synthesis, medicinal chemistry, computer-aided drug design (CADD), spectroscopic analytical techniques (HPLC, UV-Vis), and process chemistry.

## 4. Proprietary Innovations, Healthcare Initiatives & Publications at SIPTec

### "Know Your Medicine" (KYM) Public Healthcare Initiative
- SIPTec launched **"Know Your Medicine"**, an innovative free online public healthcare platform.
- Citizens and patients can enquire about medicines they consume and obtain detailed insights into indications, therapeutic effects, drug interactions, contraindications, and biochemical dissection of active ingredients.
- Users can upload images of prescriptions or medicine strips for expert evaluation, managed by qualified medical practitioners and SIPTec pharmacy faculty members.

### "The Galen" - Pharmacy E-Magazine
- SIPTec publishes **"The Galen"**, an internationally reviewed pharmacy e-magazine.
- Features latest breakthroughs in drug discovery, clinical pharmacy, regulatory updates, market trends, case studies, and faculty/student research papers.
- Guided by an advisory committee comprising prominent national and international pharmaceutical scientists.

### Patented Research: Pharmaceutical Drug Solubility Device
- SIPTec researchers developed and patented a specialized mechanical device engineered to enhance the solubility and bioavailability of poorly aqueous soluble active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs).

### "SIPTec Headstart" - Industry-Academia Forum
- Annual flagship industry-academia conclave that connects pharmaceutical industry captains, formulation scientists, and QA/QC directors directly with SIPTec students for mentorship and recruitment.

## 5. Laboratories & Research Infrastructure at SIPTec Gandhi Nagar

### Specialized Laboratories & Facilities
- **Pharmaceutics Formulation Laboratory**: State-of-the-art pilot plant setup with multi-station tablet punching, coating pans, capsule filling, and dissolution test apparatus.
- **Central Instrumentation Facility (CIF)**: Equipped with high-precision analytical equipment including UV-Visible Spectrophotometers, HPLC systems, Brookfield Viscometer, and Digital Flame Photometers.
- **Pharmaceutical Chemistry & Drug Synthesis Lab**: Advanced synthesis equipment, fume hoods, rotary evaporators, and molecular modeling workstations.
- **Pharmacology Lab & Animal Facility**: CPCSEA guideline-compliant experimental setups for drug screening and physiological modeling.
- **Pharmacognosy & Phytochemistry Lab**: Extraction units, Soxhlet apparatus, microscopes, and chromatography equipment for herbal drug evaluation.
- **Medicinal Herbal Garden**: On-campus botanical collection of therapeutic flora and medicinal plants for herbal pharmacognosy research.
- **Machine Room**: Heavy-duty industrial pilot plant equipment for industrial pharmacy training.
