## Academic Institutions under Sagar Group (SGI) in Bhopal
1. **SISTec - Gandhi Nagar**: Sagar Institute of Science and Technology (Opposite Airport, Bhopal). Flagship engineering campus with MBA and Pharmacy.
2. **SISTec-R - Ratibad**: Sagar Institute of Science Technology & Research (Ratibad, Bhadbhada Road). Dedicated engineering, automotive and farm machinery hub.
3. **SISTec-E**: Engineering wing providing advanced technical specializations.
4. **SIPTec - Gandhi Nagar**: Sagar Institute of Pharmacy and Technology (PCI and RGPV approved).
5. **SIPTec-R - Ratibad**: Sagar Institute of Pharmaceutical Technology & Research.
6. **SISTec Business School**: MBA & BBA programs focused on industry-ready management.

## Accreditations, Rankings & Recognitions
- **NAAC Accredited**: Recognized for quality education, governance, and infrastructure.
- **NBA Accredited**: Accredited for eligible engineering departments.
- **AICTE & PCI Approved**: All engineering and pharmacy courses approved by national apex councils.
- **ARIIA Ranking**: Ranked prominently in Atal Ranking of Institutions on Innovation Achievements for innovation and startup incubation.
- **Affiliation**: Rajiv Gandhi Proudyogiki Vishwavidyalaya (RGPV) for technical degrees; Barkatullah University for undergraduate business foundation.
